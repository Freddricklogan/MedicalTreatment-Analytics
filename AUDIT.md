# AUDIT — MedicalTreatment-Analytics (pre-refactor)

Audit of the previous build: `main.py` (588 lines, one script), eight
PNGs and three CSVs of its output committed under `analysis/`, a stock
image with its licence text, a notebook checkpoint, and a README whose
key findings were not computed from the committed data.

---

## A. Honesty of the copy

### A1 — "strong positive correlation (0.84) between mouse weight and tumor volume"
README line 13. The committed `Study_results.csv` (1,721 rows) is a
variant of the exercise's dataset, and on it the Capomulin
weight–volume regression gives r = −0.022 (p = 0.92, n = 24). The 0.84
is the textbook figure for the original dataset, repeated without
being run. **Fix:** the regression is computed by `stats.py` from the
committed files, the report prints r, r², p and the slope, and the
page says outright that the earlier claim was not computed from these
files.

### A2 — "Most drug regimens had mouse deaths … with the exception of Capomulin and Ramicane"
README line 14. Nothing in the script measured this, and in these
files four Capomulin and five Ramicane mice leave the study before day
45. **Fix:** Kaplan–Meier time-in-study curves per regimen with
censoring at day 45 and a log-rank test against placebo; the table
gives events per regimen.

### A3 — "Capomulin and Ramicane were the most effective"
Asserted from means. **Fix:** one-way ANOVA across the four regimens
(F = 78.5, p < 0.001) followed by Tukey HSD, which shows Capomulin and
Ramicane do *not* differ from each other at 5 % while both differ from
Infubinol and Ceftamin.

## B. Method

### B1 — Pairwise t-tests without correction
`analysis/statistical_analysis.csv` reported three "p-value vs.
Capomulin" tests and declared all significant, with no adjustment for
multiple comparisons. **Fix:** Tukey HSD adjusts across the six pairs;
the report shows adjusted p-values.

### B2 — Duplicate rule undocumented
The script printed which mouse had duplicated timepoints and dropped
it. **Fix:** `load()` returns the dropped ids (`g989`) and the page
states the rule and the count; a test pins it.

### B3 — Final timepoint selection
The script's approach was correct but untested. **Fix:**
`Study.final` is one row per mouse at its maximum timepoint, asserted
by a test (249 rows, one per mouse).

## C. Engineering

### C1 — Outputs committed, inputs hand-typed
PNGs and CSVs of results lived in the repository and drifted from the
code. **Fix:** the report is generated by `pymaceuticals report` in CI
and published; nothing generated is committed except the screenshot.
