3. Do the four regimens differ, and which pairs

One-way ANOVA on final tumour volume across Capomulin, Ramicane, Infubinol and Ceftamin: F = $anova_f, p $anova_p. Tukey HSD then asks which pairs differ at the 5 % level, with p-values adjusted for the six comparisons.

$tukey

Weight and tumour volume within Capomulin

Average tumour volume per mouse against its weight ($reg_n mice): r = $reg_r (r² $reg_r2, p $reg_p, slope $reg_slope mm³ per gram). In this dataset there is no evidence of a relationship; the earlier README's "strong positive correlation (0.84)" was not computed from these files.

4. Time in study

Kaplan-Meier curves over each mouse's last timepoint. A mouse whose record ends before day 45 counts as an event (it left the study); mice observed at day 45 are censored. The log-rank test compares each regimen with placebo.
